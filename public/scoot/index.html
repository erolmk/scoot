SCootAI@USC 2026 · working prototype

Rent a scooter from a student in your building.

SCoot lets USC students rent out their scooters and bikes to other students. You pick one up at the owner’s dorm rack, ride it, and bring it back to the same rack. The owner earns money while they’re in class.

One rental, start to finish

Maya lives in IRC and lends her scooter on weekdays at $5 / $2 / $2. Tommy lives in IRC too.

  1. 12:30 · Book. Tommy opens the app and sees the scooters at the IRC rack, each with the owner’s price and curfew (the time it must be back). He books Maya’s. He has 10 minutes to get to it, or the booking cancels for free.
  2. 12:35 · Pick up. At the rack he takes two photos: the battery display (the AI reads 84%) and the whole scooter (the “before” photo). His phone opens the padlock over Bluetooth. Billing starts.
  3. 12:45 · Park at class. He rides 0.6 miles to Leavey Library, runs the chain through the frame and a rack, and closes the padlock. Only his phone can open it now. The chain and padlock always stay with the scooter.
  4. 2:00 · Ride home. After class he unlocks it and rides back to the IRC rack.
  5. 2:10 · Return. He photographs the battery (77%), chains the scooter to the same rack, closes the padlock, and takes one photo showing the lock. The AI checks the parking and the lock and compares it with the pickup photo. Billing stops.
  6. Receipt. 1 hour 35 minutes, 1.2 miles: inside the first 2 hours and 2 miles, so $5. Maya gets $3.50, $0.75 pays off her lock, and $0.75 covers card fees and a safety fund.

Pricing

Each owner sets three prices. A typical owner charges:

Start: the first 2 hours and 2 miles$5
Each extra hour (rounded up)$2
Each extra mile (rounded up)$2
Back after the curfew$10, once

3 hours and 1 mile: $5 + $2 = $7. A 4-hour lecture plus the rides there and back (4 h 20 min, 1.2 miles): $5 + 3 × $2 = $11. Miles come from the renter’s phone GPS during the ride. Electric rides also need a $25 card hold, released after the return.

What owners do

  • Get a TTLock smart padlock and a hardened chain ($86 for both) with nothing to pay up front. There is no GPS tracker.
  • Pick the days, hours, curfew and prices. Charge the scooter themselves.
  • Keep 70% of each fare. 15% repays their lock and 15% covers card fees and a safety fund. Once the lock is repaid (about 100 rentals), they keep 85%. If they quit before that, the lock goes back to SCoot.
  • See who has the scooter and when it’s due back, not where the renter parked.

Logistics

Why a round trip?
The scooter always ends up back at the owner’s rack, so nobody has to go find it.
How does the lock open?
The padlock opens over Bluetooth from the renter’s phone. Their key works from pickup until they end the rental, even if they’re late.
What if they’re late?
They still bring it back and pay $10 once, plus any extra hours.
Who is responsible during the ride?
The renter, a signed-in USC student. Their phone records its location at every unlock and lock (the ride log).
How does the app know the battery?
The AI reads it from the pickup photo. During the ride the app subtracts the miles ridden times that scooter’s drain per mile, and the return photo corrects it.
Battery dies?
The renter kicks it home without the motor, or walks it if that isn’t safe.
Damage, theft or a crash?
The renter reports it in the app; for theft they also call DPS. If the AI flags new damage, a person compares the before and after photos and decides. The scooter stays off the app until then.
What if a photo is unclear?
The renter can retake it, or send it to a person and keep going. The review happens after, and the scooter stays off the app until it’s done.

What the AI does

Four checks, all from phone photos: the battery % (at pickup and return), the parking and lock (at return and whenever you park), new damage since pickup (at return), and the expiry date on a driver’s license (once, before your first e-scooter ride). The AI never approves a rental or charges anyone.

What’s real in this prototype

Real: the whole app and the AI photo checks. Simulated: USC sign-in, payments and the lock. To try a full rental: sign in, book Big Red, and use the demo photos at each step.
