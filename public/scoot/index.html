SCootFight On. Scoot On.

Borrow a Trojan's ride.

Scooters left on campus racks get stolen, and most sit idle while their owners are in class. SCoot gives owners a smart lock up front, at no cost to them. Their scooter or bike earns money while they're in class, and part of SCoot's cut pays off the lock.

  • Round trip. Pick it up at the owner's rack, bring it back to the same rack.
  • Only Trojans. USC NetID login, plus a license or permit check for e-scooters. Regular bikes need none.
  • AI-checked. Photos at pickup and return: battery %, parking, lock and damage.

This is a live prototype. Tap around the phone. On your own phone, open this page for the full-screen app.
