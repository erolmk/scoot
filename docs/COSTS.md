# RideOn costs (updated Nov 14 2026)

Planning estimates, not quotes. Prices checked on retailer pages on Nov 14 2026, before tax and shipping.

## Lock kit per vehicle: about $168, no monthly fee

| Part | Price | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| igloohome Padlock Lite (SP3) | $82.99 | Bluetooth padlock; the owner can send a renter a phone key with a start and end time, without sharing the owner's account. IP66. https://store-us.igloohome.co/products/padlock-lite |
| ABUS 10KS hardened chain, 3 ft (about 10 mm) | $56.39 | Goes through the frame and the rack. Cable locks get cut. https://mrlock.com/chain-3-8-thick-3ft-hardened-special-alloy |
| Apple AirTag | $29.00 | Hidden recovery tag, no monthly fee. https://www.apple.com/shop/buy-airtag/airtag |
| **Total** | **$168.38** | |

The earlier plan (igloohome Padlock 2 + chain + cellular GPS tracker) was about $317 per vehicle plus about $25 a month.

Cheaper variant: the same kit with an ABUS 8KS 8 mm chain ($38.34) is $150.33.

Limits to be honest about:
- The padlock's shackle is 6 mm. The thick chain doesn't fix that weak point. It's a pilot lock, not a certified high-security bike lock.
- The renter opens it with a time-limited key in the igloohome app. Putting that inside the RideOn app needs igloohome's business API.
- An AirTag isn't live GPS, and it can warn whoever is carrying it. It helps recovery; it doesn't guarantee it.

## Six-week pilot at IRC (5 vehicles)

| Item | Paid staff | Student volunteers |
|---|---:|---:|
| 5 lock kits | $840 | $840 |
| Outdoor charging spot (2 outlets) | $3,200 | $1,500 |
| Theft reserve (vehicles + kits) | $3,340 | $1,000 |
| Repairs | $1,000 | $500 |
| Supplies | $150 | $150 |
| Six weeks of running costs | $4,200 | $700 |
| **Total** | **about $12,700** | **about $4,700** |

Running costs with paid staff are mostly people at $20 an hour (on-call duty, charging attendance, inspections), plus an insurance placeholder of $300 a month. In the volunteer version, owners charge their own scooters and trained student volunteers take the on-call shifts. Insurance, card fees and hosting stay.

Rental fares (about $160 a month at 40 rentals) don't cover the pilot. It's a subsidized experiment.
